Privacy at Clean28

Clear about the information you share.

This policy explains how Clean28 handles details submitted through this website. Last updated 13 August 2026.

What we collectHow we use itFormSubmitKeeping informationContact us

What we collect

When you request a quote, we collect the details you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, postcode, service interest and information about your premises or cleaning needs.

How we use your information

We use your details to respond to your enquiry, prepare or discuss a cleaning quote, arrange services you request and keep a record of our communication. We do not sell your personal information.

How FormSubmit is involved

Our online quote form uses FormSubmit to relay your submission to admin@clean28.com.au. FormSubmit processes the information you enter solely to deliver the enquiry. Avoid including payment details, passwords, health information or other sensitive information in the form.

Keeping information secure

We take reasonable steps to protect enquiry information and keep it only for as long as it is needed for the purpose described above, to manage a customer relationship or to meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements. You can also ask us not to use your details for follow-up communication.

Contact us about privacy

For privacy questions or requests, email admin@clean28.com.au or call 0447 716 696.
